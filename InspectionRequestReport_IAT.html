Terms and Conditions

DescriptionDefinition / Condition
Booking TimelineInspection requests must be submitted at least 5 (five) working days before the scheduled inspection. The schedule is issued only after payment confirmation or a written agreement.
Scope of ServiceInspection using a Lux Meter & Luminance Meter. Parameters: average illuminance level, uniformity of illuminance, glare (TI), and number of lamp points lit.
Required DocumentsMust be submitted at least 3 (three) working days before the inspection: lamp specifications, road layout, number and position of lamps, and other relevant technical data. If incomplete or late, the report may not be concluded and/or a rescheduling fee may apply.
Man-Day & Additional CostsInspections are conducted at night only (19:00–24:00). 1 man-day = 1 working day of inspection. Transportation and accommodation are borne by the applicant.
Cancellation & ReschedulingCancellation with less than 1 day's notice (H-1) incurs a 100% fee. If site conditions are not ready, the full fee is still charged. Rescheduling with less than 3 working days' notice incurs an administrative fee of Rp 1,000,000.
Applicant's ObligationsEnsure the location is safe and accessible; ensure electricity and lamps function as planned; provide a field escort and technical documents.
Inspection ReportThe report is valid only for the site conditions at the time of inspection. It cannot be considered a quality certificate or a guarantee of future performance. Reproduction/publication of the report requires PT. QUALIS INDONESIA's written permission. The report may not be used for advertising or promotion.
Data Storage & Re-issued CopiesInspection data is stored per internal procedure. Re-issuing a copy of the report incurs an additional administrative fee.
Inspection Body's LiabilityClaims regarding inspection results must be made within 30 days of report issuance. Liability is limited to the service fee paid. The inspection body is not liable for financial or operational losses, or the applicant's technical decisions.
Force MajeureThe inspection body is not liable for delays or failures caused by natural disasters, government policy, extreme weather, power outages, or other emergencies.
ConfidentialityApplicant data and information are protected in accordance with the law and ISO/IEC 17020:2012, except where disclosure is legally required.
PaymentInspection fees are payable in advance or per contract. The inspection schedule will not be issued without payment.
TaxesAll taxes, levies, or duties arising from the inspection service are the applicant's responsibility.
Lien / Report RetentionPT. QUALIS INDONESIA reserves the right to withhold the inspection report if the applicant's payment obligations have not been fulfilled.
Court AppearanceIf PT. QUALIS INDONESIA staff are requested or summoned as an expert witness regarding inspection results, the applicant bears the appearance cost per the applicable rate.
Governing LawThis agreement is governed by the laws of Indonesia; disputes are resolved through the courts of Indonesia.
