Term and Conditions

DescriptionDefinition/Conditions
Booking Lead TimeInspection requests shall be made at least 5 working days prior to the scheduled date of inspection.
Pre-Shipment Inspection (PSI)PSI (Final Random Inspection / FRI) is a random sampling inspection carried out when a shipment is 100% produced and at least 80% packed. It covers type identification, function, markings, quality/workmanship, quantity, packing, unit completeness, and compliance with the agreed specification.
Initial Production Inspection (IPI)Conducted when up to 20% of the total order quantity is produced, normally at the start of the production cycle. Depending on the product and process, IPI may cover raw materials, partially completed products, and/or (as in PSI) finished products and packing. Production status is verified at this stage.
During Production Inspection (DPI)Conducted when approximately 20%–40% of the total order quantity is produced, normally at the start of the production cycle. Depending on the product and process, DPI may cover raw materials, partially completed products, and/or (as in PSI) finished products and packing. Production status is verified at this stage.
Loading SupervisionThe shipment lot is loaded into the container under supervision. Container condition, packing, quantity, and products loaded are checked to ensure correct, good-condition delivery.
Sample SealingSamples are randomly selected and sealed from a lot at a factory or other location by the inspector, for lab testing, customer reference, or other purposes. May be performed as part of an inspection or as a separate service.
Fabric InspectionA systematic fabric evaluation (fabric checking) in which defects that could affect the quality of the final garment are visually identified.
Re-InspectionThe re-inspection quantity should be equal to or less than the quantity in the previous Final Inspection. Conducted when the reworked product is 100% produced and 100% packed, unless otherwise confirmed in writing by the client.
Man-day8 working hours (09:00–18:00, including 1 hour for lunch, UTC+08:00), covering inspection, reporting, and/or travelling within a normal working day. Minimum charge: 1 man-day. Rate as agreed with the customer.
Travelling Time ChargeApplies if round-trip travelling time exceeds 4 hours. Rate: an extra 0.5 man-day for every additional 4 hours (round trip).
Travelling ExpenseCost of transportation, charged as per quotation.
AccommodationApplies if travel is required the day before or after the inspection, or the inspection requires 2 or more days to complete. Charged as per quotation.
PostagePostage for samples and document delivery as requested by the customer; collected on delivery.
OvertimeHours worked outside normal working hours (09:00–18:00, UTC+08:00). Rate as agreed with the customer: man-hour = man-day rate / 8 hours × 1.5.
Working/Travelling on HolidaysWeekends and local public holidays. Saturday: 1.5× the agreed man-day rate. Sunday & public holidays: 2× the agreed man-day rate.
Abortive Inspection ChargeQUALIS will abort an inspection, loading supervision, sample sealing, or other customized service on arrival if conditions do not meet the agreed requirements (e.g. goods not 100% produced/packed as required). QUALIS will wait up to 2 hours to contact the customer before aborting. Charge: man-day rate × number of inspectors, plus any expenses incurred.
Late BookingFor online bookings received after 12:00pm on the working day before the requested inspection date, inspection cannot be guaranteed and is subject to manpower availability. An administrative fee of USD 80 applies unless instructed otherwise by QUALIS.
Late CancellationFor cancellation or rescheduling received after 12:00pm on the working day before the confirmed inspection date, an administrative fee of USD 80 applies unless instructed otherwise by QUALIS.
